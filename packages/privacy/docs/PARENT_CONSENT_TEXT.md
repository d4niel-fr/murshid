### Hello. [Student preferred name] has asked to use Murshid.

Murshid is a study coach app. It helps students plan their week, keep track of deadlines and goals, and find activities and competitions. Because [Student preferred name] is under 18, we need your permission first.

**Please read each item and switch on only what you agree to.** You can change any of these at any time from the link in our emails, and [Student preferred name] can switch them off too.

**1. Use Murshid (needed for anything else)** — ☐
Murshid stores what [Student preferred name] types in: profile, goals, tasks, achievements and CV drafts. It uses AI to give study tips and plans. It will **not** write graded work for them.

**2. Connect to school** — ☐
Murshid can read [Student preferred name]'s classes, assignments, due dates and class notes from the school's learning platform. It can only read; it cannot post, submit or change anything.

**3. Weekly summary to you** — ☐
We email you a short weekly summary: tasks done and deadlines coming up. You will **not** see their private messages or notes.

**4. Reminders** — ☐
Murshid can send [Student preferred name] reminders by app notification, email or WhatsApp.

**5. Sharing with universities and programmes** — ☐
Only when [Student preferred name] applies to, or asks to hear from, a specific university or programme, Murshid can send them the profile items your child picks. Nothing is shared without that step.

**What we never do:** sell data, show ads, use your child's data for marketing, or share it with anyone not listed above.

**Your rights:** you can download a copy of your child's data, ask us to correct it, or delete the account at any time. Full details are in our [Privacy Policy].

By tapping **Confirm**, you confirm that you are [Student preferred name]'s parent or legal guardian and agree to the items you switched on.
