## 1. Who we are
Murshid ("we", "us") is a study and goals coach app for secondary-school students in the UAE, run by [legal entity name, licence no., address]. Contact our privacy team at [privacy@…].

## 2. Who can use Murshid
- Murshid is for students **aged 13 and over**. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.
- Students **under 18** can only switch on Murshid's features after a **parent or guardian** has verified their email and given consent.
- We ask for your date of birth on a neutral screen (it does not tell you an age limit), to set your account up correctly.

## 3. What we collect, and why
Each purpose below is **off until you (and, if you're under 18, your guardian) turn it on**, and each can be turned off separately at any time in Settings.

| Purpose | What data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Core service** | Name, preferred name, date of birth, grade, school, curriculum, emirate, languages, interests, strengths, goals, tasks, achievements, CV drafts | To coach you, plan your week and track your goals |
| **School connector** | Class names, assignments, due dates, class notes and announcements from your school's learning platform (read-only) | So your plan reflects real deadlines |
| **Guardian summaries** | A weekly summary of progress (tasks done, upcoming deadlines). **Never** your messages to Murshid or your notes | To keep your guardian in the loop |
| **Notifications** | Device push token, email, WhatsApp number (if you add one) | Reminders and briefings |
| **Sharing with universities / programmes** | Only the profile items you choose, only with the named organisation you choose | When you apply or ask to be contacted |

We also keep **security and accountability records**: consent records (what was agreed, by whom, when), an access log (which part of Murshid read which kind of data, when), and an AI-use log per task (see §5).

## 4. What we never do
- We **never sell** your data.
- We **never use your data for advertising** or profiling for commercial purposes, and we show no ads.
- We never share your data with your school, guardian or anyone else beyond the purposes above.
- We never store your school or Google/Microsoft passwords. Connections use the provider's sign-in, and access tokens are kept in an encrypted vault.

## 5. AI and academic integrity
- Murshid uses AI models to help you plan, explain topics and give feedback. Murshid is **a coach, not a ghostwriter**: it will not write assessed work for you.
- Each task has an **AI-use level** (none / support / led), matching your school's rules. When a task is "none", Murshid only helps with planning and reminders.
- We keep a log of AI help per task (what kind of help, not your full messages) so you can make an honest AI-use declaration.

## 8. Your rights
You (and your guardian, if you are under 18) can:
- **See and download** all your data (Settings → Download my data — a ZIP of JSON files).
- **Correct** it.
- **Withdraw consent** for any purpose, any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing does not affect your access to other features that don't need that purpose.
- **Delete** your account and all its data (Settings → Delete account). We delete everything and keep only a one-way fingerprint showing that a deletion happened.
- **Complain** to us at [contact], and to the UAE Data Office. **[LEGAL CHECK: competent authority.]**

## 9. Safety
Murshid follows the UAE Child Digital Safety law (Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2025): privacy-protective defaults, age checks, guardian tools and no commercial use of children's data. If you see something harmful or a safety issue, use "Report" in the app or contact [safety contact].

## 10. Changes
