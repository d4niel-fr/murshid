# Murshid: feature list

## For schools (main focus)
- School licence per student per year (AED 120–180, list AED 150), with its own school tenant. School data never mixes with family accounts.
- Sign in with Microsoft (Microsoft Entra ID) for teachers, school admins and students. Connect Microsoft 365 once and Teams classes, members and assignments sync. Simulated in the demo build; the connector is ready for real Microsoft credentials.
- Teachers can post assignments directly in the Murshid portal. Each one appears in every student's plan and Today list straight away.
- Class progress report: per student, work handed in (%), average mark, overdue and remaining work, the next 7 days (tests, sport, events, how full the week is), and an at-risk / keep-an-eye / on-track / ahead status with the reason.
- Who needs what: suggested groups for the next task (needs support, same task with more time, core, extension), each student with a short reason, plus peer partners and mixed-attainment groups.
- Best day to set homework: on-time likelihood for every set-day and due-day option, the best one in plain words (e.g. "Best day to set: Tuesday, due Thursday: 87% likely on time"), what it avoids, and a day-by-day class workload chart. The task length can be changed.
- Weekly reports: every student's report is sent automatically each Friday at 4 pm (work done, overdue, still to do, tests coming up, hours of sport and activities) and rolls up for the teacher.
- School & licence page: licence, seats, join code, Microsoft 365 status, classes, and families vs schools shown separately.
- Explainable by design: every figure comes from fixed rules on the school's own data, and each screen shows how it was worked out.

## For students
- One plan for every class, assignment, test, sport fixture and activity.
- School page: their school, classes and teachers, work from teachers (new items highlighted), and their own weekly report.
- Ask Murshid: "plan my week around my mid-term and both sports" builds a full week with protected revision, training and rest. Progress shows in plain steps such as "Checking your timetable…".
- Progress charts for every subject and sport across the year.
- Opportunities matched to the student (competitions, programmes, internships, volunteering), saved with a task to start the application.
- LinkedIn-ready CV and portfolio exported as a PDF (parent review reminder for under-18s).
- English and Arabic, light and dark themes, works on desktop and iPad.

## For families
- "Get Murshid" chooser: I'm a parent / I'm a student / My school provides Murshid.
- Parent plan: a parent buys Murshid for their child (Plus AED 29 a month or AED 249 a year; Pro AED 79 a month or AED 699 a year) and gets a weekly summary.
- Students can buy Murshid for themselves and link a school later.

## Privacy and safety
- Separate tenants for each school and each family. A teacher only sees classes they teach, in their own school.
- Every change made for a student is logged in the school's audit log.
- Synthetic demo data only: every student and teacher in the demo is fictional.
