# Murshid: product description

## Long version (about 150 words)

Murshid helps students get their work done and stay on top of it. Every class, assignment, test, sport fixture and activity sits in one plan, and Murshid builds each student's week around it.

Schools are our main focus. A school licenses Murshid for its students (AED 120–180 per student per year). Teachers post work in the Murshid portal, or the school connects Microsoft 365 so staff and students sign in with Microsoft and Teams classes come in automatically. Whatever a teacher sets lands in every student's plan straight away.

Teachers get the full picture: a class progress report with at-risk flags, suggestions for who needs support, more time or extension work, the best day to set homework with the class's on-time likelihood, and a weekly report from every student. Families can also buy Murshid directly. We are proposing a Grade 11 pilot at Our Own High School Al Warqa'a in Dubai (GEMS Education).
