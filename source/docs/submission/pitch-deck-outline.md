# Murshid: pitch deck outline (6 slides)

Positioning: schools first. Plain language throughout; every number below comes from the product's demo data or our pricing.

## 1. Murshid helps students get their work done
- One plan for every class, assignment, test, sport fixture and activity.
- Murshid for Schools: every student on top of their work, every teacher with the full picture.
- Visual: the student's School page beside the teacher's class progress report.

## 2. The problem
- Students juggle work from six or more subjects, tests, sport and activities, with no single view of their week.
- Teachers set homework without seeing what else their class has due, so deadlines pile up on the same days.
- Large schools have very few counsellors per student (about 1 per 930 at our school, where we propose the pilot), so struggling students are spotted late.

## 3. How it works
- The school licenses Murshid for its students. Families can also buy it directly (Plus AED 29 a month, Pro AED 79 a month).
- Teachers post work in the Murshid portal, or the school connects Microsoft 365: staff and students sign in with Microsoft and Teams classes and assignments sync.
- Whatever a teacher sets lands in every student's plan straight away.
- School and family accounts are separate: a school only ever sees students under its own licence.
- Visual: the "Get Murshid" chooser (parent / student / my school) and Connect Microsoft 365.

## 4. What teachers get
- Class progress report: work handed in, overdue and remaining for every student, using their workload across all subjects, with at-risk flags and the reason for each.
- Who needs what: support, the same task with more time, core, or extension work, plus peer partners and mixed groups.
- Best day to set homework: for example "Set Tuesday, due Thursday: 87% likely on time", with a day-by-day chart of how full the class's week is.
- Weekly reports that every student sends automatically each Friday, rolled up for the teacher.
- Every number follows fixed, explainable rules on the school's own data.

## 5. Traction and business model
- Proposed Grade 11 pilot at Our Own High School Al Warqa'a, Dubai (GEMS Education), subject to the school's approval.
- Schools: AED 150 per student per year list price (range AED 120–180); a 1,300-student secondary school is about AED 156k–195k a year.
- Families: Plus AED 29 a month or AED 249 a year; Pro AED 79 a month or AED 699 a year.
- Next: run the proposed pilot, then approach other GEMS schools and UAE school groups.

- Close: "Murshid for Schools. Every student on top of their work."
