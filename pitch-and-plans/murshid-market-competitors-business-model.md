# Murshid: market, competitors and business model (as of 10 Oct 2026)

**Short answer:** Sell to both, in a set order. Don't start with B2C alone or B2B alone.
1. **Now:** run a free pilot at Our Own High School (OOW). Universities and programmes pay for clearly labelled listings, the way BridgeU works.
2. **Revenue proof by Jan 2027:** a paid parent tier (freemium).
3. **2027–28:** a per-student school licence sold through GEMS.
4. **Later:** a government (B2G) licence, following Alef Education's path.

The reasons: schools already get guidance software free or cheap; parents already spend heavily on tutoring; and universities already pay to reach counsellors and students.

**The gap Murshid fills:** these tools are split three ways. Guidance platforms serve the counsellor and the application. AI tutors serve subjects. Planners handle tasks. None of them acts as a single "manager": one agent that holds a student's goals, notes, study plan, opportunity search, applications, CV/portfolio and LinkedIn presence, and that also reports to parents. Local players (Alef, Abwaab, Noon) focus on curriculum.

## 3. Recommended pricing (all figures are estimates)

- **B2C freemium:** Free tier covers goals, a planner, and limited AI.
  - **Plus: AED 29/mo or AED 249/yr.** Gross margin ~70–77% at USD 1.5 of AI cost a month.
  - **Pro (application season): AED 79/mo or AED 699/yr.** Includes CV/portfolio, application tracking, and one human review a term. Margin ~85–90% before the human review.
  - Plus costs about what one hour of tutoring costs in a whole year.
- **B2B (Grades 9–12): list price AED 120–180/student/yr. GEMS group price ~AED 100.**
  - **Warning:** at Naviance-like prices (AED 75), gross margin is only ~23% with USD 1.5/month AI cost over 10 months. At AED 150 it is ~61%.
  - So either sell Murshid as replacing two tools (a guidance platform plus an AI tutor), or bring model cost under USD 0.75/month by sending simple requests to cheaper models.
- **B2G (KHDA/MoE/ADEK):** about AED 50–80/student at scale, plus a fixed fee to adapt it to local requirements. Alef's ADEK contract (renewed to 2033) shows this route can work, but it takes 12–24 months.
- **University and programme partners:**
  - Labelled "featured" listings at AED 1,500–5,000/month. For comparison, one listing platform charges AED 500/month for a founding sponsor (https://www.vistabylara.com/ai-tools/university-pathway-planner/comparison).
  - Plus **AED 50–200 per enquiry that a parent has agreed to share**.
  - Universities already pay **USD 4,400** for 26 meetings with Gulf counsellors (https://bmiglobaled.com/recruit-students/gulf-india-counsellor-workshop).
  - Rules: paid placement must never change Murshid's recommendations, and no student data is shared without parental consent (UAE data-protection law covers minors).
- **Unit economics (estimates):**
  - Freemium conversion: 3–5%.
  - Acquisition cost for each paying parent: AED 60–150, through ambassadors and short-video content.
  - Plus subscriber lifetime value: about 9 months × AED 29 × 75% margin ≈ **AED 195**. That gives an LTV/CAC of roughly 1.3–3×. Annual plans and the Pro tier lift it.
  - School example: 1,300 Grade 9–12 students × AED 100 = **AED 130k a year**, with a sales cycle of 3–9 months.

## 4. Go-to-market (Oct 2026 → Jan 2027)

- **Oct:** Get the principal and counsellors to approve. Sign parental consent forms. Run a beta with 30 Grade 11–12 students at OOW.
- **Nov:** Widen to a 150–300 student pilot (Grades 9–12). Recruit 10 student ambassadors with referral codes. Post 3 Instagram/TikTok videos a week (study plans, finding opportunities, before/after CVs).
- **Dec:** Launch paid Plus. Pitch GEMS central (45 UAE schools, ~146k students: https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/education-industry/dubais-gems-sees-dip-in-uae-school-registrations-but-growth-plans-intact/). Get letters of intent from 2–3 Dubai branch campuses or summer programmes.
- **Counsellor networks:**
  - UAE Counselor's WhatsApp group (contact list in the THE database: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/cms-academic/sites/default/files/2024-02/Counselor%20%26%20University%20Support%20Database.pdf)
  - International ACAC MENA Regional Institute
  - THE/BMI Gulf & India Counsellor Workshop
  - ADEK Counselors Forum
- **Jan 2027 metrics for judges:**
  - Number of active pilot users, plus 4-week retention above 40%
  - Number of study plans and CVs created
  - Opportunities matched and applications submitted
  - Counsellor hours saved, from a survey
  - Parent NPS
  - Paid conversion %
  - Signed school or university letters of intent
  - AI cost per active student (target under USD 1)

*Gaps:* KHDA hasn't published a 2025-26 Grade 9–12 split (2024-25 is the latest). Crimson, Unifrog, Cialfo, BridgeU-for-universities and Univariety publish no full pricing. GCC and tutoring market figures come from analyst reports.
